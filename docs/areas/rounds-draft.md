# Draft: how a club "round" works (for the owner to react to)

Status: DRAFT from the 11 survey answers (2026-11-12). Nothing built. Not a decision yet.

## What the survey said (11 people)
- Cadence: 8 want every week, 1 every two weeks, 2 "whenever". 7 watched almost every week of the old club.
- Biggest obstacle: not enough time (7). Forgetting (2). Only 1 said skipping had no consequence.
- Loved most: seeing everyone's ratings (10), discovering movies (10), getting together (9), debates (7), rating (6), not knowing the movie (6), choosing on your turn (6).
- Points game: 6 love it, 3 sound fun, 2 don't care, 0 against.
- Prize: pick the next theme (8), hall of fame (7), bragging rights (6), cash pot (5).
- Notifications: all 11 want all of them. 8 are "very open" to unknown movies, 3 "somewhat".
- Avoided genres: foreign-language (5), horror (3), war (2), musicals (2). 4 said none. Horror is also liked by 7, so tastes are split.
- "Must have" answers: full participation / commitment (5, incl. Dave: 90% buy-in on ground rules). Others: Razzie (worst movie) night, monthly highlight reel of best/worst reviews, GIF reviews, a 75% Rotten Tomatoes floor.

## Proposed rules
1. **Weekly round, flexible.** One movie a week. Skipping is allowed but deliberate ("skip this week" button), so it is a choice, not forgetting.
2. **Old tradition kept:** a random year (1960 on) is drawn, the next member in rotation picks from that year's Best Picture nominees.
3. **Plex fallback:** if fewer than 2 nominees are on Plex (via Seerr check), the picker may choose any movie from that year with a decent score (75%+ on the ratings we already show).
4. **Picker rotation:** fixed order, everyone once per ~11 weeks. If the picker has not picked by the deadline, the next person goes.
5. **Heads-up tags, not bans:** a pick shows a warning if it is a genre someone avoided (foreign-language is the big one). Picker can still choose it.
6. **Weekly rhythm (days to be chosen):** pick announced, about a week to watch, rate by the deadline, ratings hidden until the reveal, then discussion.
7. **"Watched" = you rated it.** Simple and needs no Plex link. Plex "finished" could later fill it in automatically.
8. **Points:** 1 for rating on time, +1 for a written review or GIF, picker bonus when the group average is high. No penalty for skipping.
9. **Prize:** the monthly winner picks the next month's theme and goes in the Hall of Fame. Cash pot left out of the app (family can do it on the side).
10. **Extras from the write-ins:** end-of-month highlight reel (best and worst reviews); a yearly "Razzie night" (guess the Razzie winner from titles, or watch one); GIF reviews.
11. **Ground rules vote:** members approve the rules in the app; 90% (10 of 11) to adopt, per Dave.
12. **Notifications:** new pick, your turn to pick, reminder 2 days before the deadline, ratings revealed.

## Open questions
- Keep "random year + Best Picture" as the main format, or loosen it? The survey did not ask.
- What day does the week start, and how long to watch?
- Should the group average of the pick matter, or only participation?
- Do we want 75% as a floor, a warning, or nothing?

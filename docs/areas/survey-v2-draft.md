# Survey v2 (DRAFT, not live)

Proposed replacement for the 19 questions in survey.md. The live survey is unchanged until the owner approves this. Types: multi = select all, single = pick one, text = free text. Every question optional except the name.

Intro: Hobson Movie Club is coming back as an app on your phone. Old way: each week a random year from 1960 on, the Best Picture nominees for that year, the next member picks one, we watch it and rate it out of 5 stars, then everyone's ratings are revealed. Before we build it we want to know what worked and what didn't. About 3 minutes.

1. What movie genres do you enjoy? (multi, same list as before + Other)
2. Any genres you would rather NOT watch? (multi, same list as before + None + Other)
3. What are your TOP 3 all-time favorite movies? (text x3)
4. How open are you to movies you have never heard of? (single, same 4 options as before)
5. What did you enjoy MOST about the old club? (multi) Reason to get together as a family; Not knowing what the movie would be; Discovering movies I'd never have watched; Choosing from the list when it was my turn; Rating the movie; Seeing everyone's ratings; The debates and disagreements
6. How often did you actually watch the week's movie? (single) Almost every week; About half the weeks; Only now and then; Hardly ever
7. What usually got in the way? (multi) Not enough time; The movie didn't interest me; I forgot; Nothing happened if I skipped it; Other
8. How often would you realistically take part now? (single) Every week; Every two weeks; Once a month; Whenever my schedule allows
9. Would you like a points game? Points for watching and rating, bonus points when others rate your pick highly. (single) Love it; Sounds fun; Don't care either way; I'd rather not have scoring
10. What should the points winner get? (multi) Bragging rights; Pick the next movie night's theme; A small cash pot everyone chips into; Their name in a hall of fame; Nothing, the points are enough; Other
11. Would you install the club on your phone and get notifications (your turn to pick, ratings due, results are in)? (single) Yes, and I'd like all of them; Yes, but only a few notifications; I'd rather get a text or email; No thanks
12. What should the club MUST do to work this time? (text)
13. Any other ideas or changes? (text)

## What changed from v1 and why
- Dropped (the owner can add back): Rotten Tomatoes thresholds, decades, submit-your-own-favorites, who picks, group discussion, revisiting movies, hall of fame question, awards list. These ask people to design the club; better to react to a concrete plan later.
- Added: 6, 7, 9, 10, 11. These answer the real problems (people not watching, no reason to) and the app plan.
- Open design point: Best Picture nominees are not always on Plex (a project rule). Needs a decision before the picking feature.
